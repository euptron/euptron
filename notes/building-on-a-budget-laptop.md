Every new coding session was a decision that required proper planning.

When your device can't brute-force its way through inefficiency, you're forced to actually understand what your tools are doing instead of throwing memory at the problem.

The OPPO A1K had **2GB of RAM** and coding on it was excruciatingly slow. Although coding on mobile device is feasible, I quickly hit a hardware and UX ceiling. I needed something that could handle:

- Real project structures
- Real linting
- Real CI hooks


So I built it.

## The hardest part

The hardest part wasn't the code. It was accepting that progress would be slow, and that **slow is not the same as worse**.

> Every crash taught me something a faster machine would have let me skip past.

I wouldn't recommend the constraint to anyone by choice, but I also wouldn't trade what it taught me.
